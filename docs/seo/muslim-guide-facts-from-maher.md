# Muslim guide — facts from Maher (2026-11-17)

Authoritative source for the guide page. Maher lives in Playa del Carmen; these
override the web research in muslim-guide-research.md. Scope: Cancún + Playa del
Carmen only (Tulum: Amanah only goes there for the ruins — no Tulum section).

## Mosques & community
- **Playa del Carmen — Masjid Al-Rahman**, Calle 3 Sur, C. 60 Diag. Sur con, Ejidal, 77712.
  A real, big mosque. Imam from Al-Azhar (Egypt). Tarawih + iftar in Ramadan.
  Year-round Islamic classes for adults and kids, Quran classes. Activities and
  celebrations for both Eids.
- **Cancún — Masjid Al-Rahim**, C. Alce 24, 77500 Cancún. A musalla (prayer room).
- **Emara Academy** — da'wa, classes and activities for Muslims and new converts.
- Muslims living in Cancún + Playa + Tulum: roughly 250–300.

## Halal food — Playa del Carmen
- **French Kebab** — Muslim-owned, only halal food.
- **Ar Rayan** — Muslim-owned, only halal food.
- **Street Food Latino** — serves halal meat; owner is not Muslim.
- **Shawarma** and **Los Faraones Tacos & Kebab Express** — halal meat, but may sell beer.
- **Falafel Nessya** — falafel.
- Kosher places: Moshe Cube Kosher, Two Smokers Smokehouse, Let's Meat Kosher Steak House.
- Plenty of seafood and fish options.
- Cancún: Maher knows no halal restaurant.

## Halal meat
One halal meat provider in Playa del Carmen — the owner of Ar Rayan and French Kebab.
Ask at French Kebab for halal meat and they provide what you need.

## Swimwear
No clear law about it. Hijab is fine everywhere; people are very respectful, so the
burkini is fine everywhere (beaches, cenotes, parks).

## Hotels
No halal-certified hotel. You can ask for no alcohol in the room, but hotel food won't
be halal. Good alternatives: Airbnb, or resort condos like our partner TutCasa in
Mareazul (a splendid condo).

## Amanah tours for Muslim families
All tours are designed for Muslims: private, so no shared transport with strangers.
When something must be shared (e.g. a snorkeling boat, where a private one would be
very expensive for the family), there is no alcohol and no music. All tours are
Muslim-friendly and we provide a prayer carpet if guests want to pray during the
tour. We do NOT provide food during tours.
